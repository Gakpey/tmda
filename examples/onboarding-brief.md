# First-run onboarding - Brief

Task file: [onboarding.tmda](./onboarding.tmda) - open it to see tracked progress.

> This document is the *why*; `onboarding.tmda` is the *what*, one checkbox per
> piece of work.

## What we want to do

Give a brand new account something to do in its first five minutes. Today a
new workspace opens onto an empty project list and most people simply leave.

We want a short, dismissible checklist that reflects real state - it should
tick itself as the user completes things rather than asking them to tick it.

## Welcome checklist

The checklist has four entries: invite a teammate, create a first project,
connect one integration, and read the two-minute overview. Each entry links
straight to the screen that completes it.

## Why it matters

Activation in the first session is the strongest predictor we have of a
workspace surviving its first month. This is the cheapest lever we have on it.

## Constraints and non-goals

- No new backend surfaces; the checklist is derived from existing events.
- Must be fully ignorable - it is not a gate on anything.

## What "done" looks like

- A new workspace shows the checklist without a page reload.
- Completing an item ticks it on the next event, not on refresh.
- The checklist disappears permanently once all four items are done.
